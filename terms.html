Terms & Conditions

1. Car Rental

All vehicle rentals are subject to availability and confirmation by Ashwing VIP. A refundable deposit of $200 USD / R3500 ZAR is required at the time of booking.

2. Deposit & Payment

The deposit secures your reservation and is fully refundable if cancellation is made 48 hours or more before the scheduled pickup. Late cancellations may incur a partial or full deposit charge.

3. Driver Requirements

  • Valid driver's license held for a minimum of 2 years
  • Minimum age: 23 years
  • Additional drivers must be registered at pickup

4. Fuel & Mileage

Vehicles are supplied with a full tank of fuel and must be returned with a full tank. Mileage limits may apply depending on the rental group. Excess mileage will be charged at prevailing rates.

5. Insurance

All rentals include third-party liability insurance. The renter is responsible for the first $500 / R8500 of any damage (excess). Reduced excess options are available at an additional cost.

6. Vehicle Condition

The vehicle must be returned in the same condition as received. Normal wear and tear is accepted. Any damage, interior soiling, or mechanical issues caused during the rental period will be charged to the renter.

7. Chauffeur & Shuttle Services

Our chauffeur and shuttle services operate on a scheduled basis. Waiting time beyond 15 minutes from the agreed pickup time may incur additional charges. Ashwing VIP is not liable for delays caused by traffic, weather, or circumstances beyond our control.

8. Prohibited Use

  • No smoking in any vehicle (fine of $100 / R1700 applies)
  • No off-road driving
  • No use of the vehicle for illegal activities
  • No sub-letting or rental to third parties

9. Limitation of Liability

Ashwing VIP shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from the use or inability to use our services. Our total liability is limited to the amount paid for the specific service.

These terms are governed by the laws of Zimbabwe. Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Harare, Zimbabwe.
